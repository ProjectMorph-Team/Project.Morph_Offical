# Contributing

Project Morph is currently maintained as a controlled architectural and provenance-sensitive repository.

## Contribution Rule

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

- you have the right to submit the contribution;
- your contribution may be licensed under the Sustainable Development Licence (SDL) v1.0;
- your contribution preserves all copyright, authorship, provenance, and licence notices;
- your contribution does not remove or weaken the Ground Truth Manifest;
- your contribution does not misrepresent Project Morph authorship or origin;
- your contribution does not create commercial use rights unless separately agreed in writing.

## Authorship

Contributions may be acknowledged, but contribution does not transfer primary origin, creator status, project ownership, trademark rights, or commercial implementation rights.

## Commercial Contributions

Commercial, institutional, platform, or implementation contributions require prior written agreement with the Licensor.
